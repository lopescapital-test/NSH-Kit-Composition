§1 · The Pivot

What changed May 2

Five shifts in the kit architecture, four from Kyle's May 2 review and one (Rezzimax → optional upgrade) added in this build. All flow from one decision: get the Class 3B medical laser out of the foundational kit and replace it with something a parent can safely operate without certification.

Class 3B medical laser leased to family ($8,000-class device)
→
LED Laser NSH ($300 cost / $1,100 sold) as foundational anchor
Class 3B included in all paid tiers by default
→
Class 3B as parent-certified upgrade, available at any tier
Saebo TENS as clinician-prescribed add-on
→
SaeboStim Pro 4-in-1 ($279) graduates to foundational
Rezzimax in foundational kit (every paid tier got one)
→
Rezzimax becomes optional upgrade — Saebo 4-in-1 covers most universal use
qEEG only as in-clinic intensive offering
→
qEEG enters as premium leased home offering
The new architecture in one line

Foundational kit → clinician add-ons → parent-certified upgrades

Every paid tier gets the same foundational kit. The protocol prescribed on top of that kit is what differs by profile and intensity (see Profile + Intensity Map v0.2). Add-on devices are pulled from a defined library when the case calls for them. The Class 3B laser and qEEG sit in a separate upgrade tier any family can opt into with parent certification.

This is what the v0.5 protocol always said about delivery — same starting point, personalized progression, tiered support. The kit just got cleaner.

Why this matters for liability and scaling. A $300 LED laser parents can use without certification removes the largest single regulatory and operational drag on scaling NeuroHome to 50+ families. Class 3B stays available — but as an opt-in with explicit parent training and documented certification, not the default.

§3 · The Bloodwork

Labs — what we're looking for

Six things behind every developmental challenge we treat. The lab panel reframes the test names into what parents actually want to know: what's driving their child's struggle. Two tiers of panel scope cover the six categories at different depths.

The six categories

What labs are looking for, plain language.

① Food allergies — hidden reactions driving inflammation and behavior flares. ② Hidden infections — parasites, candida, viruses, strep, bacteria. ③ Environmental toxins — heavy metals, plastics, PFAS, industrial chemicals. ④ Mold exposures — mycotoxins from water-damaged buildings or contaminated food. ⑤ Nutritional deficiencies — vitamins, minerals, antioxidants, amino acid metabolism. ⑥ Metabolic imbalance — mitochondrial energy production, neurotransmitter pathways, detox capacity.

Every paid tier includes labs. Universal labs cover four of six categories; full diagnostic covers all six. Profile assignment (per Profile + Intensity Map v0.2) determines which panel a case starts with.

Why labs are universal across paid tiers. The Profile + Intensity Map (v0.2) showed cohort cases consistently had biomedical drivers behind the neurological presentation. Treating the brain without checking the gut, the toxin load, or the metabolic markers misses the upstream cause in most cases. Labs aren't a tier-graded extra — they're a clinical prerequisite. The tier difference is panel scope (universal vs. full), not labs vs. no-labs.
§4 · The Library

Clinician-prescribed add-ons

Add-ons are pulled from a defined library when the case calls for them. The clinician prescribes; the family acquires; the device is layered onto the foundational protocol. Six items in active rotation, plus the affiliate-sourced items families can buy directly.

How add-ons get assigned. Add-on selection follows from profile (§4) and intensity (Profile + Intensity Map v0.2). A Profile A child running HIGH intensity gets the heaviest add-on stack; a Profile D child running LIGHT typically gets none. The clinician makes the call after the initial assessment and articulates it in the homecare prescription.

§6 · The Premium Layer

Premium upgrades

Three upgrade offerings. All available as add-ons at any tier (Foundation, Accelerated, Immersive). Class 3B and qEEG require explicit parent training and documented certification before deployment. Rezzimax is a non-certified optional upgrade — added if a family wants the additional vagal/vibration tooling beyond what the foundational kit delivers via Saebo + LED Laser NSH.
